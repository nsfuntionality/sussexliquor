1. Sign in to YouTube with the Google account you want to own the channel (sukh286@gmail.com or a dedicated one).
2. Click your avatar → **Settings** → **Add or manage your channel(s)** → **Create a channel**.
   - Create it as a **Brand Account** (this keeps the channel separate from your personal identity — essential for faceless content, and lets you add managers later without sharing your password).
3. Name it **TeslaTracking**.
4. Claim the handle: **@TeslaTracking** (fallbacks if taken: @TeslaTrackingHQ, @TeslaTrackingNews, @TeslaTrackingDaily).

7. YouTube Studio → Settings → Channel → **Advanced**: set "No, it's not made for kids" as the default.

## 6. Content system (the repeatable engine)

**Cadence:** 2 long-form (2-min) videos/week + 2–3 Shorts cut from them. Consistency beats volume.

**Weekly pipeline (what we did today, repeatable in ~1 hour per video):**
1. Pick ONE story from @SawyerMerritt / Tesla officials on X (I research and verify via web search).
2. 12-block script → voiceover (same "Sterling" voice = channel identity) → clips (AI + free-license stock + text cards) → assemble.
3. Upload package: title options, thumbnail A/B, description with chapters, .srt captions, pinned comment.

**Formats to rotate:**
- BREAKING: single news story (like video #1)
- EXPLAINED: evergreen educational ("What is FSD actually?", "How Robotaxi geofences work")
- NUMBERS: quarterly deliveries/energy in 2 minutes
- VS: Tesla vs Waymo style comparisons

**Playlists to create on day one:** Robotaxi Rollout · FSD Explained · Tesla News This Week · Shorts

## 7. Consistency rules (what makes the channel feel like one brand)

## 8. Growth & monetization roadmap

- **Phase 1 (0→1k subs):** Shorts are the discovery engine — every long-form gets 2–3 Shorts. Titles target search: include "Tesla", "FSD", "Robotaxi" naturally.
- **Phase 2 (YouTube Partner Program):** requires 1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch-hours (12 mo) or 10M Shorts views (90 days). 2-min videos = watch-hours build slowly; Shorts route is realistic first.
- **Phase 3:** memberships, affiliate links (Tesla referral, gear), sponsorships (EV/tech products).
- **Costs:** at ~260 credits per fully-produced video, your 3,000/mo Ultra plan supports ~10–11 videos/month — matching the 2/week cadence with headroom.
